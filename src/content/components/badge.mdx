Use `<Badge>` to highlight status, versions, or categories inline within text or headings.

## Common patterns

Place badges next to a page heading to show the status of a feature:

{/* An accessible demo heading avoids adding an example to the page TOC. */}
<div role="heading" aria-level={2} className="mb-[1.125rem] mt-9 font-heading text-2xl font-semibold tracking-[-0.025em]">
  Webhooks <Badge variant="warning">Beta</Badge>
</div>
